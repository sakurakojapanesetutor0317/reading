🌐 English Translation

Obon Holiday

It is August.

In Japan, there is an Obon holiday.

Obon is an important time for families.

During Obon, ancestors return home.

Mr. Tanaka returns to his family home by bullet train.

He eats a meal with his family.

They also eat watermelon.

They go to the grave together.

They clean the grave.

After that, they talk with their family.

Obon is an important time for families.
