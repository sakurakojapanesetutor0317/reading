🌐 English Translation

A Hot Day

Today is July.

It is very hot every day.

Alex is in Japan.

This morning, I went to the park with a friend.

However, it was very hot.

The two of them talked, saying, "It's hot, isn't it?"

After that, they went to a convenience store.

They bought cold tea and ice cream.

They returned home.

They turned on the air conditioner.

They drank cold tea.

At night, they will watch fireworks with a friend.

They are looking forward to it very much.
